Official Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

1. Overview & Nature of Application

streamvaultexio is a clean, standalone media player utility designed for iOS. The application functions solely as an empty client media player capable of rendering user-provided media streams.

Important: streamvaultexio does not host, provide, scrape, broadcast, index, or distribute any media, video, or stream content. All media content accessed through the application is directly and exclusively supplied by the individual end user.

2. Information Collection & Use

We respect your right to privacy. The application is built on a zero-data-collection philosophy:

  • No Personal Information Collected: streamvaultexio does not collect, record, transmit, or sell your name, email address, physical location, phone number, or device identifiers.
  • No Third-Party Analytics or Tracking: The application contains no advertising SDKs, no behavioral tracking frameworks, and no third-party telemetry libraries.
  • Local Storage Only: All configurations, favorite channels, custom tags, and connection endpoints you enter are stored locally on your device and never transmitted to our servers.

3. Network Connections & Playback

When you play a stream within streamvaultexio, the app establishes a direct, client-to-host network connection between your device and the stream URL specified by you. None of your playback history, stream traffic, or viewing activity passes through or is logged by streamvaultexio.

4. App Permissions

The application requests only technical system capabilities required for playback operation:

  • Network Access: Required solely to stream media from user-configured endpoints.
  • Background Audio: Used to continue playing audio streams when the screen is locked or while multitasking.

5. Children's Privacy

streamvaultexio does not knowingly collect or solicit any information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EU). Because no data is collected from any user, children’s privacy is inherently safeguarded.

Any future revisions to this Privacy Policy will be posted directly to this page with an updated effective date.
